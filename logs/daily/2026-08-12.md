# Official Bounty Reroll Mechanics Captured

The in-game Bounty Board documentation confirmed that reroll cost increases together with the chance of higher-tier Bounties.

| Reroll # | Cost | Basic | Intermediate | Advanced | Master |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1–3 | 10 slips | 35% | 50% | 13% | 2% |
| 4–6 | 20 slips | 20% | 60% | 17% | 3% |
| 7–8 | 30 slips | 0% | 60% | 36% | 4% |
| 9–10 | 100 slips | 0% | 0% | 92% | 8% |

### AxieOS Optimizer Implication

Higher reroll cost is not simply a penalty.

The optimizer must consider:

- current task BP
- current task type
- reroll number
- next reroll cost
- tier probability distribution
- Fortune Slip balance
- expected BP of possible replacement tasks

This replaces the earlier assumption that all rerolls should be evaluated using the same probability distribution.

# AxieOS Reporting Issues Identified

## 1. Cross-Currency Marketplace Economics

The daily report currently tries to compare:

- Regular Choco purchase cost in **RON**
- listing/sale proceeds in **ETH**

directly.

This produces meaningless output such as:

- negative RON spread
- -100% ROI

### Required Fix

Normalize both sides into a common currency before computing:

- spread
- ROI
- realized profit
- break-even price

Potential reference currencies:

- WETH
- USD
- PHP

---

## 2. Partial Marketplace Lots

The Aug 12 Regular Choco lot demonstrates the need for partial-lot accounting.

Actual state:

**15 listed → 13 sold → 2 remain**

The current marketplace reporting logic does not yet represent this cleanly.

### Required Improvement

Track:

- original quantity
- quantity sold
- quantity remaining
- weighted realized proceeds
- remaining cost basis

## 3. Released Axies as Terminal Events

Still pending from Aug 11:

Marketplace reporting should understand:

**Buy → Release = CLOSED / CONSUMED**

and not keep released Axies as open trading positions.
