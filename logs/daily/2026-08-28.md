# 17. Bounty Capital-Preservation Rule

August 28 continues to follow the established AxieOS operating principle:

```text
Bounty requires purchase
        ↓
buy only required quantity
        ↓
qualify for task
        ↓
consume only what another task requires
        ↓
preserve or relist remaining inventory when economical
```

The objective is not simply to maximize BP.

The objective is:

```text

+

-

-

gas
```
