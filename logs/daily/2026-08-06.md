# Terrarium Buff Strategy Observation

The August 5 delayed activation showed the operational risk of relying on manual daily buff activation.

Because Terrarium buffs are stackable and purchasing additional duration simply extends the countdown timer, a weekly activation schedule may reduce repetitive work and prevent accidental unbuffed periods.

Potential future routine:

- Weekly buff day: **Monday**
- Add approximately **7 days** of duration
- Maintain a safety buffer before expiry
- Record:
  - Activation time
  - Remaining duration before renewal
  - New duration added
  - Fortune Slip cost
  - Whether snapshot was taken while fully buffed

This can be evaluated further before permanently changing from daily to weekly activation.

# Reflection

August 6 demonstrated three useful AxieOS strategy patterns:

1. **Low-value Bounty tasks can justify immediate rerolls.**
   Two 10-slip rerolls produced an additional 1,080 BP.

2. **Inventory should sometimes be retained instead of immediately relisted.**
   The newly purchased Regular Choco was needed for another task, avoiding unnecessary marketplace transactions.

3. **Operational consistency matters for Terrariums.**
   The recovery in rank after August 5 shows that several hours without buffs can materially distort snapshot comparisons.

The next improvement should be recording exact Terrarium buff activation times and evaluating whether a recurring 7-day buff schedule is more efficient than daily activation.
