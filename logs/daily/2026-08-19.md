# 13. Strategy Architecture

Current AxieOS optimizer strategy modes continue to evolve toward differentiated resource policies.

The key design principle is:

```text
same bounty board
+
different strategic objective
=
different optimal action plan
```

Examples:

### Conserve

Optimize for:

* Low capital usage
* Fortune Slip preservation
* Inventory preservation
* Efficient BP

### Rank Push

Optimize for:

* Higher expected BP
* Increased reroll willingness
* Controlled reserve depletion
* Maintaining some Fortune Slip safety buffer

This allows the optimizer to respond to weekly leaderboard conditions instead of applying one fixed policy every day.
