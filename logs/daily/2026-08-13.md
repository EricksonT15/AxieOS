# Optimizer Observations

## 1. Task Overlap Created Significant Value

The strongest execution overlap was:

- Task 1 — 600 BP
- Task 3 — 650 BP

One Premium Choco feed completed both.

**1 consumable → 1,250 BP**

This reinforces the need for the future Bounty optimizer to evaluate combinations of tasks rather than treating every task independently.

---

## 2. Purchase-and-Relist Strategy Worked Again

Task 6 required buying Premium Choco.

Instead of using the newly purchased Choco:

1. Bought the required asset.
2. Received Bounty completion.
3. Immediately listed it.
4. Asset sold one minute later.
5. Existing inventory was used for the actual feed tasks.

Therefore the economic cost of Task 6 was largely the marketplace trading fee rather than the full consumable value.

Future AxieOS optimization should distinguish:

- **Purchase cost**
- **Recoverable capital**
- **Marketplace fee**
- **Net Bounty execution cost**

## 4. Official Reroll Tier Effects Matter

Today's Task 6 moved from:

- Rerolls 1–3: **10 slips each**
- Rerolls 4–6: **20 slips each**

The higher-cost tier has a better probability distribution for higher-difficulty tasks, but the additional cost must be evaluated against:

- current BP
- completion cost
- task overlap
- Fortune Slip reserve
- expected BP improvement
- probability of getting a better tier
- probability of receiving an expensive or undesirable task

Starting BP alone is not enough.

## 5. Inventory Reserve Strategy

Opening 10 Regular Lucky Pouches was deliberately more than the 3 required by the Bounty.

Reason:

- Regular Choco usable inventory would have fallen to zero after today's Feed-5 task without replenishment.
- Future Bounties frequently require Feed-5 or Feed-10 Regular Choco.

- **<10 usable:** Low inventory / replenish
- **10–14 usable:** Minimum operating reserve
- **15–20 usable:** Preferred reserve
- **>20 usable:** Potential excess / resale

# Development Observation

The daily marketplace report currently treats:

- WETH purchase
- ETH listing

as different currencies and therefore displays:

- Target gross spread: N/A
- Target gross ROI: N/A

This protection is correct for genuine cross-currency trades such as RON → ETH.

However, for marketplace economics, **ETH and WETH should normally be treated as equivalent units for spread/ROI calculations**, while still preserving the distinction in the raw transaction records.

Potential future improvement:

> Add an ETH/WETH equivalence rule to marketplace reporting without weakening the existing cross-currency guard.
