# Operational Observation — Time Has Economic Value

August 7 provides a useful example for AxieOS.

The 90 BP Classic task was not necessarily bad under normal circumstances.

However, because there was no available time to play Classic, its expected realized reward was effectively close to:

- **0 BP**

Spending 20 slips to eventually obtain the 400 BP Dojo task was therefore a rational decision.
