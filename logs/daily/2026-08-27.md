# 9. Terrarium Mechanics — Official Formula

Official Terrarium mechanics captured on August 27 established:

```text id="femxor"
Individual Axie Flame
=
Base Atia's Flame
+
Evolved Part Boost
+
Accessory Boost
```

Plot Flame is then:

```text id="xj5fu0"
Plot Flame
=
floor[
sum of all working Axies' individual Flame
×
(1 + Land Item Boost %)
×
(1 + Fortune Slips Buff)
]
```

This should now be treated as the canonical Terrarium Flame formula in AxieOS.
