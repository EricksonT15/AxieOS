# 7. Capital-Recovery Interpretation

The Premium Cocochoco transaction should not be evaluated as a standalone speculative trade.

Its purpose was:

```text
complete 680 BP bounty
```

The proper economic framework is:

```text
Bounty Economics
=
680 BP utility
+
net WETH recovered from resale
-
original purchase cost
-
marketplace selling fee
-
user-paid gas
```

Therefore the strategy objective was not necessarily to generate a trading profit.

The objective was:

```text
complete high-value bounty
+
minimize permanent capital loss
```

This distinction should remain explicit in AxieOS accounting.

# 19. Marketplace Lifecycle Rule

For bounty-related consumable transactions, AxieOS should track each purchased unit through:

```text
acquisition
        ↓
bounty qualification
        ↓
consume / hold / list
        ↓
sale
        ↓
realized recovery
```

This prevents an item from simultaneously appearing as:

```text
usable inventory
and
sold inventory
```

after disposal.
